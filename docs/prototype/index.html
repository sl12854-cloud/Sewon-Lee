How we score dates

Data sources

  • Weather (live): Open-Meteo. Dates within 16 days use the forecast. Later dates use the 10-year average for that week (2016–2025).
  • Public holidays (live): Nager.Date, including regional Balearic holidays for Ibiza and Lunar New Year and Chuseok for Seoul.
  • NYC events (live): NYC Open Data permitted events for the next month, and the historical permits from the same week last year for later dates.
  • Festivals: a curated list of recurring festivals. Dates marked Typical dates follow the usual pattern and change every year, so confirm before booking.
  • Flights and hotels: live prices open on Google Flights and Booking.com with your dates filled in. EventStay doesn't show real prices on this page yet.

Scores

  • Crowd level (0–100): a typical tourism-season level for the city, plus weekends, festivals, public holidays and, in New York, permit counts. Quiet < 35 · Moderate < 55 · Busy < 75 · Very busy 75+.
  • Hotel price level: an estimated seasonal index × weekend, festival and holiday effects, shown against the 12-month average. It is not a quote.
  • Best-dates score: combines crowd, price level and weather comfort, weighted by what matters most to you.
Prototype disclaimer. Crowd levels, hotel price levels and festival dates are EventStay estimates based on typical seasons and recurring events, not guarantees. Weather, holidays and NYC permit data come live from public APIs with no API key. Prices open on third-party sites. EventStay doesn't take bookings or collect personal information.
